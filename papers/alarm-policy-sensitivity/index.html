Alarm-Policy Sensitivity in Bearing Endpoint Warning: A Conveyor-Motivated Dataset Audit

Nguyễn Hoàng Phụng · NexaEvo, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

Abstract

Bearing-warning benchmarks can yield different conclusions depending on whether they credit newly issued notifications or persistent alarm states. We investigate this distinction through a targeted audit of eight maintenance dataset families and reproducible experiments on complete XJTU-SY and PRONOSTIA histories. Five fixed comparators are evaluated with whole-asset separation and training-only transformations. On fifteen XJTU-SY bearings under condition holdout, the original fifteen-minute protocol gives RMS thresholding 8/15 timely onset hits but 14/15 active-state-overlap hits; logistic regression gives 5/15 and 11/15, respectively. A complete horizon–threshold grid and causal persistence analysis show substantial policy sensitivity without selecting a favorable operating point. A paired disjoint-calibration stress-test changes logistic coverage and notification burden together. In a separate within-PRONOSTIA evaluation of six complete learning histories, RMS and logistic achieve 5/6 and 6/6 onset hits, but produce 4.208 and 10.760 early episodes per monitored hour. The second dataset is not a cross-dataset transfer test. Raw provenance, timestamp exceptions, predictions, configurations and independent computational checks accompany the analysis. The experiments concern laboratory acquisition endpoints, not independently adjudicated factory failures. Their contribution is an auditable case study showing why event coverage, notification frequency, persistent-state occupancy and endpoint construction must be interpreted jointly.

Key results

8 → 14

RMS thresholding on 15 XJTU-SY bearings (H = 15 min, q = .95): 8/15 timely onset hits, 14/15 active-state-overlap hits, from the same scores. Logistic regression: 5/15 and 11/15.

37 → 13

A causal two-confirmation rule cuts logistic out-of-horizon episodes from 37 to 13; its onset hits stay at 5/15.

11 → 16

Disjoint threshold selection raises logistic hits from 11/30 to 16/30 test appearances, and out-of-horizon notifications from 27 to 48.

10.76 / h

On six complete PRONOSTIA histories, logistic reaches 6/6 onset hits with 10.760 early episodes per monitored hour; RMS 5/6 with 4.208.
